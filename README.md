# Juritas Signal

Ambient institutional intelligence — a living operational twin that surfaces the single risk path an enterprise must act on first.

## What it is

Juritas Signal is an interactive demo of an enterprise digital twin concept. It ingests contracts, vendor agreements, policies, and governance frameworks, then reasons across them to surface hidden obligations, vendor exposure, and operational risk as one coherent signal — not a dashboard.

The experience walks through six phases:

1. **Landing** — introduction to the intelligence concept
2. **Ingest** — document ingestion builds the institutional model (41 entities, 28 connections)
3. **Twin** — the enterprise appears as a living relationship map across vendors, obligations, policies, and dependencies
4. **Simulate** — choose a scenario and trace it through trigger → reasoning → dependencies → exposure → propagation → consequences → interventions
5. **Vulnerability intelligence** — three causal risk signals surface from the twin's model
6. **Outcome** — one urgent operational signal emerges

### Simulation scenarios

- GDPR Investigation
- Cybersecurity Breach
- Supplier Failure
- Regulatory Audit
- AI Governance Violation
- Insider Risk
